Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

HowThingsWork Uploader ("the app") is a private script used only by its creator to upload and manage videos on the creator's own YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app accesses:

  • the channel's name and its list of uploaded videos (titles, IDs, privacy settings), to avoid duplicate uploads;
  • the ability to upload videos and to set their title, description, tags, privacy and scheduled publish time.

The app does not access comments, subscribers, viewer data, analytics, payment information or any other Google account data.

How the data is used and stored

  • The data is used only to upload and schedule the creator's own videos and manage their privacy settings.
  • The OAuth token and a list of uploaded video IDs and titles are stored only on the creator's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties, and none is used for advertising.
  • The app collects no data from viewers or anyone else, and uses no cookies or tracking.

Keeping and deleting data

The locally stored data stays until the creator deletes it. To revoke the app's access at any time, go to the Google security settings page (myaccount.google.com/permissions) and remove HowThingsWork Uploader. Revoking access stops the app from reading or changing the channel. To ask for any stored data to be deleted, email the contact address below; it will be deleted within 7 days.
